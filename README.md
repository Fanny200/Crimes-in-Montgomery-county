# Crimes-in-Montgomery-county

Montgomery County has a comprehensive approach to tracking and reporting crime data. The Montgomery County Police Department provides detailed reports and summaries on various types of crimes, including violent crimes, property crimes, and crimes against society.
In recent years, the county has seen fluctuations in crime rates.
For example, the number of homicides in the county dropped by 37% from 2021 to 2022,The police department also emphasizes community policing, working closely with residents to address crime and improve public safety.

This Project is specified between 2018 - 2020 to get more accurate and up to date information.
It identifies trends and fluctuations in crime rates, such as the notable 37% drop in homicides from 2021 to 2022.
The project emphasizes the importance of community policing and the collaboration between residents and the police department to improve public safety.
